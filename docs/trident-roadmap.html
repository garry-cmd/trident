Assumptions & Risks

  • Hardware ships within 1–2 weeks of order. Actisense and Peplink are generally in stock; Cerbo GX availability should be verified before ordering.
  • Irene is accessible for boat work throughout the timeline. If she's hauled out or in transit, hardware phases shift.
  • N2K backbone is healthy and has open T-connectors for the NGT-1 and Cerbo drops. If not, additional N2K cabling is needed.
  • The Pi 5 running Signal K + Next.js + tile server stays within thermal limits in the below-decks install. The aluminum case helps but monitor temps during the sea trial.
  • Blue Latitude O-Charts license allows activation on the Pi. Their 2-device limit may require one activation on Pi and one on laptop — verify before purchase.
  • The biggest schedule risk is KR3 (Trident app). It's 16 tasks of custom code over 2 weeks. If it slips, KR4 (chart view) absorbs the impact. Chart view can ship after deploy as an update.
  • Keeply sync (stretch) can begin in parallel with KR3/KR4 if momentum is good, but it doesn't gate the deploy.
